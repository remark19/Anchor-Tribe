# Anchor-Tribe
Escape Algorithmic Noise. Build Real Consistency with Your Tribe.

Tired of toxic feeds and superficial social media? Anchor & Tribe places you in a private pod of 4 people to achieve tangible goals through verified micro-artifacts and offline connection.

Designed For Our Generation's Real Needs
Every feature is engineered to foster deep focus, strict accountability, and genuine human connection.

Tribe Pods (4/4)
No thousands of followers. You are matched into trusted pods of 4 peers sharing your exact growth track.
Verification Quests
No fake streaks. Protect your daily streak by uploading genuine artifacts—code snippets, study logs, or project notes.
Focus Room & Audio
Built-in Pomodoro timer paired with ambient soundscapes (Rainfall, Coffee Shop, Waves) for deep focus blocks.
AI Burnout Coach
An objective retrospective coach that summarizes pod progress, highlights friction, and prevents fatigue.
